## Executive Summary

Our company expects to receive a €8,000,000 payment from a European customer at a future settlement date. Because the payment will be received in euros but reported in U.S. dollars, changes in the EUR/USD exchange rate could significantly affect the final value of the receivable. If the euro depreciates before settlement, the company will receive fewer U.S. dollars than expected, reducing revenue and potentially affecting profitability. This memo outlines the foreign exchange exposure, summarizes three potential hedging strategies, and recommends continuing with a structured evaluation before selecting the most appropriate hedge.

## Background & Objectives

The company is exposed to foreign exchange risk because it has a future euro-denominated receivable that must eventually be converted into U.S. dollars. Although the expected payment amount is known, the future exchange rate is uncertain. Even relatively small movements in the EUR/USD exchange rate could have a meaningful financial impact on an €8,000,000 transaction. The objective of this project is to evaluate practical hedging alternatives that reduce exchange rate uncertainty while supporting the company's financial planning and risk management objectives.

## Methods

Three hedge families will be evaluated. A forward contract locks in an exchange rate today, providing certainty but eliminating any benefit if the euro strengthens. A money market hedge uses borrowing and investing to create a synthetic hedge, although it requires access to financing and involves additional transactions. Currency options provide downside protection while allowing the company to benefit if the euro appreciates, but the option premium increases the overall cost. Each alternative will be analyzed using consistent assumptions to compare expected outcomes, costs, and risk.

## Limitations & Next Steps

This memo serves as the initial planning document and does not yet include live market data or a recommended hedge. In the next phases, I will develop the hedge specification, build and audit the analytical model, incorporate current exchange rate information, validate the calculations, and prepare a final recommendation for management approval.
